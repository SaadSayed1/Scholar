Video Editing

This comprehensive Video Editing Course is designed for aspiring editors, storytellers, and filmmakers. From mastering basic techniques to learning advanced editing tricks, this course covers everything you need to become a skilled video editor.

Our hands-on training program uses industry-standard software like Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and DaVinci Resolve. By the end of the course, you’ll be equipped to edit professional-quality videos for social media, YouTube, corporate projects, or personal projects.


Course Curriculum


  • Introduction to Video Editing: Basics, software overview, and setting up your workspace
  • Editing Essentials: Timeline editing, cutting, and trimming techniques
  • Transitions and Effects: Using transitions, applying effects, and creating custom animations
  • Color Correction and Grading: Adjusting color, lighting, and enhancing the visual tone
  • Audio Editing: Syncing audio, adjusting levels, and using sound effects and music
  • Text and Titles: Adding text, creating lower thirds, and designing titles
  • Exporting Projects: Export settings, formats, and delivering high-quality videos
  • Final Project: Create and edit a short video project from start to finish

Course Details

  • Duration: 8 Weeks (2 sessions per week)
  • Price: ₹20,000
  • Requirements: Basic computer knowledge; no prior video editing experience required
  • Certification: Upon completion, students will receive a Certificate of Completion.
