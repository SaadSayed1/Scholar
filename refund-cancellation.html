Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective Date: 13th January 2025

Thank you for choosing Scholar! We want to ensure that our customers have a smooth experience with our courses. Below is our policy regarding cancellations and refunds.


1. Course Cancellation


You can cancel your course purchase at any time before accessing the course materials.

If you cancel your order before you have accessed any of the course content, you will be eligible for a full refund as outlined below.

Once you start accessing the course content, your purchase is considered final, and cancellation will not be accepted unless specified under our refund policy.


2. Refund Eligibility


Full Refund: If you cancel your purchase within 7 days of the transaction and have not accessed the course material, you will be eligible for a full refund.

Partial Refund: If you request a refund within 7 days of purchase but have accessed some or all of the course materials, a partial refund may be provided at our discretion. This is based on the amount of content you have accessed.

No Refund: After 7 days from the date of purchase, no refund requests will be accepted, except in cases of technical issues or other exceptional circumstances, which will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.


3. How to Request a Refund


To request a refund, please contact our support team by sending an email to [Insert contact email] with the following details:


  • Your full name

  • Order ID or proof of purchase

  • Reason for cancellation/refund request

Our team will review your request and process the refund as quickly as possible, usually within 5-7 business days.


4. Refund Method


Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. If that payment method is no longer available or valid, we will work with you to find an alternative method.


5. Non-Refundable Items


Subscription fees for memberships or courses that are billed on a recurring basis are non-refundable after the billing cycle has started.

Any courses or digital products that have been downloaded or accessed in full will not be eligible for a refund.


6. Technical Issues


If you experience technical difficulties while accessing your course, please contact our support team immediately. We will do our best to resolve the issue as soon as possible. If the issue cannot be resolved and you are unable to access the course, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund, depending on the situation.


7. Changes to the Refund Policy


Scholar reserves the right to update or modify this Cancellation and Refund Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Your continued use of our services after any changes to this policy will constitute your acceptance of the revised terms.
