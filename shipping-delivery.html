Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective Date: 13th January 2025

At Scholar, we strive to deliver a seamless experience for all our customers. This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines the process of delivery for any physical items or merchandise ordered through our website. Please note that all of our courses and digital content are available for instant access upon payment, and therefore are not subject to shipping.


1. Delivery of Physical Products


If you order any physical products such as books, merchandise, or other items from our website, we will process and ship your order as quickly as possible. Below are the key details regarding the shipping and delivery process:


  • Processing Time: Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed during weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

  • Shipping Methods: We offer a variety of shipping methods including standard, expedited, and express shipping. The shipping method available to you will depend on your location.

  • Shipping Charges: Shipping charges will be calculated at checkout based on your selected shipping method and delivery address. You will be informed of the total shipping cost before completing your purchase.

  • Delivery Time: Estimated delivery times depend on your location and the shipping method selected. Domestic orders typically arrive within 5-7 business days, while international orders may take up to 14-21 business days.

  • Tracking Information: Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with tracking information so you can monitor the progress of your delivery.


2. Shipping Restrictions


  • International Shipping: We offer international shipping to many countries. However, some regions may not be eligible for delivery. Please check during checkout if your country is eligible for shipping.

  • Shipping to P.O. Boxes: We do not currently ship to P.O. boxes or APO/FPO addresses. Please provide a valid street address for shipping.


3. Order Issues or Delays


In case of any issues with your order, such as delays, missing items, or damaged products, please contact our customer support team immediately at [Insert contact email]. We will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.


4. Return and Exchange


For information on returns and exchanges, please refer to our Return and Exchange Policy. Please note that digital products, including courses and e-books, are not eligible for return or exchange once the purchase is completed.


5. Changes to Shipping and Delivery Policy


Scholar reserves the right to update or modify this Shipping and Delivery Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Your continued use of our services after any changes to this policy will constitute your acceptance of the revised terms.
